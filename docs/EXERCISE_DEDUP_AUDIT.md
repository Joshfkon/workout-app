## How to work through this report

1. **Regenerate** with real data (command at the top).
2. For each group, confirm the ✅ survivor is the one you want to keep. The
   survivor's per-exercise settings (rep ranges, YouTube video, bodyweight type)
   win; the duplicates' conflicting settings are dropped (reported by the merge).
3. **Dry-run** the suggested command and read the counts (set logs, template and
   mesocycle references that will move).
4. If it looks right, re-run with `--execute`. Duplicates are **soft-deleted**
   (`deleted_at` + `merged_into`), so a merge is reversible — see
   `unmerge_exercise(id)` — until you purge them.
5. Do **one group at a time**. Nothing here merges automatically.
